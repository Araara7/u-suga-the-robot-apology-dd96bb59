## Entry

> I'm sorry I watered your laptop instead of the fern — in my defence, they were both blinking and only one of them complained.
>
> I learned to check for leaves before pouring, and from now on I will water only things that photosynthesise.

`main.py` writes exactly these two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.
